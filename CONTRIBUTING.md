# Contribution Guidelines

Contributions are welcome! Please follow these guidelines:

- Ensure the resource is specifically related to Gemma models or the Gemma ecosystem.
- Only suggest awesome resources: tools, libraries, models, demos, tutorials, or research papers you have verified.
- Search the list first to ensure the item is not already included.
- Add your entry to the bottom of the relevant category.
- Use the format: `- [Item](URL) - Short description ending with a period.`
- Keep descriptions factual, objective, and free from promotional claims.
- We focus on high effort and high quality resources and reserve the right to reject any submission.
